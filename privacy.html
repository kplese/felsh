Privacy Policy

Applies to all Felsh Watch Faces apps for Wear OS, including Abyss Diver and Glowtube Clock. Last updated: 26 September 2026.

No data collected

Our watch faces do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or device data. They contain no code of their own, no analytics, no advertising and no network access.

Complications

Complications (for example battery, steps, heart rate or world clock) display data supplied on your watch by Wear OS or by other apps you choose. That data is shown on screen only and never leaves your device through our watch faces. The providing apps are governed by their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Our watch faces are not directed at children and collect no information from anyone.
