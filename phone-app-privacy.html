Privacy Policy – Felsh phone apps

Applies to the phone apps that come with Felsh watch faces on Google Play. Each one helps you install its watch face on your Wear OS watch and shows our other watch faces. Last updated: 28 October 2026.

What the phone app sends

To see where installing a watch face goes wrong, the phone app sends anonymous usage counts to our database (Google Firebase, stored in the EU). Each count contains only:

  • what happened, for example "Install on watch" tapped, install sent or failed, a help guide or our other watch faces opened;
  • which watch face app sent it, and its version;
  • whether a watch was connected, its type (Galaxy, Pixel or other) and how many;
  • which watch apps are installed on the phone (for example Galaxy Wearable or Pixel Watch), so the app can show the right help;
  • the phone's language setting and Android version;
  • a random code made each time you open the app, so the counts of one visit can be read together. It is never saved on your phone, so the next visit gets a new one and visits cannot be linked.

What we never collect

No name, Google account, e-mail, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID, or any device or install identifier. The counts cannot be linked to you or to your phone. We use them only to improve the app: never for advertising, never sold, never shared. We delete old counts once we no longer need them.

List of watch faces

The app downloads the list of our watch faces from kplese.github.io (GitHub Pages). It sends nothing about you to get it.

Internet services

When the phone app connects to Google Firebase or GitHub Pages, those services see your phone's IP address for that request, as with any website. We do not receive or store it.

Permissions

The phone app asks for no permissions. It uses internet access, which Android grants without a prompt, and the Wear OS connection your watch app already set up.

The watch face itself

The watch face that runs on your watch collects nothing and has no network access. Complications show data from your watch or from apps you choose, on screen only.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children and collect no information that identifies anyone.
